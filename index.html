Our Purpose

Why Nitch Foundation?

Nitch Foundation (Next-generation Innovation & Technology Community Hub) is building a new generation of Tanzanian technology creators. We engage young people with proven technology skills as volunteer mentors, guiding children and youth from primary school through secondary school, college, and university. From their first steps, learners receive ongoing mentorship to help them grow into capable engineers and technology professionals who build solutions for their communities instead of only using technology.

With guidance from skilled mentors and hands-on projects, learners grow their technology expertise and engineering skills over time, then apply them to real community challenges.

      Who We Are

      About Nitch Foundation

      Learn how we mentor children and youth from primary school through college and university to become skilled technology creators and engineers.

      Who We Are

      Nitch Foundation is a Tanzania-focused youth technology and innovation organization. We began our work in September 2026 to help children and youth become technology creators, not just users. From primary school through secondary school and advanced-level studies to college and university, skilled young people volunteer as mentors, supporting learners throughout their journey toward becoming capable engineers and technology professionals.

      What we focus on

      We grow practical skills in systems design and engineering, software, AI, cybersecurity, IoT, hardware, and emerging technologies, starting in primary school and continuing through college and university.

      How we work

      Through Builders Clubs, workshops, and hands-on projects, young people with technology skills volunteer to mentor children and youth from primary school through college and university. They provide ongoing guidance as learners develop expertise and build solutions for local needs.

      Our Vision

      Long-Term Aspiration
      "To make Tanzania a nation that invents technology, not merely consumes it."

      What this means: We want Tanzania to be a place where people and communities have the skills and opportunity to create, adapt, and lead the technologies they need—not only use technologies created elsewhere.

      Our Mission

      Action-Driven Purpose
      "To nurture a new generation of Tanzanian engineers and technology creators through continuous mentorship from primary school to college and university—so they build technology, not just use it."

      What this means: We build this generation by engaging skilled young technology volunteers to mentor learners continuously, from primary school through college and university. Together they develop expertise in AI, cybersecurity, IoT, hardware, software, and emerging technologies—and the engineering skills to create solutions for Tanzania.

      Organization Framework & Core Philosophy

      Public-Benefit Non-Profit

      Dedicated entirely to community development, youth capacity building, and sustainable innovation across Tanzania and Africa.

      Our Open Source Strategy

      Driving Open-Source Emerging Technologies for Africa

      Advancing open and emerging technology across Africa

      The Challenge We Are Tackling

      Africa should not only use technology; it should also create open-source solutions. From artificial intelligence (AI) and the Internet of Things (IoT) to cybersecurity and emerging technologies, we need open tools that protect our data, address local challenges and empower African innovators.

      Our Core Technology Pillars

      Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Local Data

      We develop open-source Swahili AI systems, speech recognition tools and AI applications for agriculture, health and education.

      Cybersecurity & Digital Safety

      We build open-source cybersecurity tools, teach communities how to stay safe online and help protect economic data.

      Internet of Things (IoT) & Hardware

      We design IoT systems that connect sensors and electronic devices to support smart agriculture, water management and energy solutions.

      Emerging Technologies

      We research and develop cloud computing, automation and data science tools suited to African contexts.
